## OTE
In an effort to support each student during their transition to the U of M and on their path to graduation, Orientation & Transition Experiences offers high-quality, dynamic experiences that cultivate community and empower students to champion their own development and leverage campus resources.
[Learn More About OTE](https://ote.umn.edu/node/26)

## & STUDENT SUPPORTERS
Orientation & Transition Experiences (OTE) is here to support you and your students’ transition to the U of M. Our Family & Student Supporter Orientation Program provides an introduction to the University’s departments integral in a students transition as well as the academic expectations of the University.
